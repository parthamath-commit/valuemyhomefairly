Questions

Is this the County Auditor?

No. Value My Home Fairly is an independent service based in Cincinnati, not affiliated with any county, Auditor or Board of Revision.

Are you lawyers or appraisers?

No. We prepare factual information from public records so you can file your own complaint. We don’t file for you, represent you or give legal advice. For complex or commercial cases we’ll point you to an attorney.

Could appealing raise my value?

Yes. The Board can keep, lower or raise a value. That’s why the check tells you when not to file.

How much will I save?

It depends on how far your value is above the market and on your local tax rate. As a rough guide, each $50,000 of overvaluation costs about $600–$900 a year in most Ohio counties. Your free check gives you your own estimate.

When can I file?

Between January 1 and March 31, with your county Board of Revision. Join the list and we’ll remind you.

What happens to my information?

We use your address to look up public property records and your email to send your result and reminders. We never sell or share it, and we’ll delete it on request.
