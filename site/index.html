What is Value My Home Fairly?

Your property tax is based on the value your County Auditor puts on your home, not on what it would actually sell for. If that value is too high, you overpay every year until it's corrected. Ohio lets every owner challenge it, but most people don't know whether their value is wrong or how to prove it.

What you get:

  • A free check of your county value against 3–5 recent sales of similar homes nearby, plus a scan of your county record for errors such as wrong square footage.
  • An honest verdict: likely too high, borderline, or fair. If it's fair, we tell you not to file.
  • A $59 evidence packet if an appeal looks worth it: comparable-sales table, complaint-form (DTE 1) entries filled in, filing checklist and a short hearing script. You sign and file it yourself.
  • A $149 option that adds a 30-minute call to walk through your case and prepare you for the hearing.
